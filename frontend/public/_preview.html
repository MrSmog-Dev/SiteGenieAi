Our Story

Built on Soil
and Stories

Verdant & Vine was born from a simple observation: the most memorable homes feel whole—where the interior aesthetics, lifestyle, and outdoor space speak the same language. Too often, gardens are treated as afterthoughts: the last thing homeowners think about, or worse, delegated to whoever's available. We saw homeowners craving something different—a garden that felt as intentional as their home itself.

Founded by a landscape architect and a plantsperson, Verdant & Vine began with two core convictions: that gardens should be designed with rigor (not sentiment), and that long-term maintenance matters as much as the grand unveiling. A beautiful rendering is one thing; a thriving, gorgeous garden three years later is another.

Over the past decade, we've worked with hundreds of homeowners, from small urban courtyards to sprawling suburban escapes. We've learned that the best gardens emerge from genuine partnership—where we listen deeply, understand how you live, design with constraint and intention, and then stick around to tend what we've created. Your garden isn't a project we complete; it's a space we steward.

Today, Verdant & Vine is a close team of designers, horticulturists, and craftspeople united by one belief: that thoughtfully designed, expertly maintained gardens change how we feel at home. We're grateful for the homeowners who've trusted us with theirs.

Common Questions

Questions We Hear

Honest answers to the things homeowners ask most. If yours isn't here, we'd love to hear from you directly.

We begin with a detailed consultation—visiting your home, understanding your vision, learning how you live and entertain, and assessing the site's light, soil, and existing conditions. We'll develop a design concept (usually 2–3 weeks), present options, and refine based on your feedback. The full design process, from initial brief to finished planting plan, typically takes 4–8 weeks. We move thoughtfully, not hastily.

We do both. Many clients work with us for "garden editing"—a focused redesign of specific zones or a thoughtful simplification of an overgrown space. Sometimes the most elegant solution is refinement, not replacement. We'll assess what's working and what isn't, then recommend whether that means fresh design, strategic renovation, or enhanced maintenance. There's no one answer; it depends on your home, your goals, and your budget.

We choose plants based on your site's specific conditions (light, soil, water availability) and your garden's design intent. We favor plants that are regionally appropriate, pest-resistant, and beautiful across seasons. We're not doctrinaire about native-only or exotic-only; rather, we build palettes that thrive in your climate and look intentional year-round. Our designs tend toward restrained, architectural plant choices rather than high-density color displays.

Design fees are upfront and fixed—typically $3,500 to $8,000 depending on site size and complexity. Installation costs vary widely (small courtyards start around $8,000; larger projects often exceed $30,000) and depend on hardscape materials, plant quantities, and site conditions. We provide transparent estimates before any work begins. We're also happy to discuss phased approaches if budget is a consideration; gardens can evolve in stages.

Most of our gardens are designed to be beautiful and relatively low-maintenance—they're not wild meadows or high-demanding perennial borders. Seasonal maintenance visits (spring renewal, summer monitoring, autumn cleanup, winter structure) keep things thriving and ensure the design matures intentionally. Many clients appreciate having us handle this; others prefer to manage basic tasks themselves once they understand the rhythm. We can custom-fit a maintenance plan to your preference and lifestyle.
